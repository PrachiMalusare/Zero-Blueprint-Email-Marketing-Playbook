# Sequence map

This map describes the strategic function of the preserved emails. It does not rewrite their content.

| Email | Primary job | Reader question addressed | Intended next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Launch announcement | Introduce the program, limited cohort and deadline | What is being launched, and why should I notice? | Reply or send a direct message |
| 2. Purpose and credibility | Explain why the program exists and establish authority | Why are Steve and John qualified, and why now? | Reply to assess fit |
| 3. Origin and transformation | Connect the founders' realization to the program outcome | What change does the program help create? | Express interest for self or team |
| 4. Program outcomes | Make the curriculum and expected work more concrete | What will I learn and do? | Request more information |
| 5. Experience, support and bonuses | Show delivery format, support system and enrollment value | What happens inside, and what support will I receive? | Reply for more information |

## Narrative progression

```text
Announcement → Purpose → Transformation → Method → Support and decision
```

The progression is useful because it does not ask one email to carry the complete sale. Each message removes a different form of uncertainty.

## Content-preservation rule

Any future edit should be proposed in a separate review document. The source PDF should remain unchanged so the original sequence and any proposed variation can be compared honestly.
