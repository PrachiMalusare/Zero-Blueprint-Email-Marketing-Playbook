# Implementation checklist

## Before launch

- [ ] Preserve and archive the approved source sequence.
- [ ] Confirm program name, dates, cohort capacity, price and availability.
- [ ] Confirm every mentor, bonus and claim is current and approved.
- [ ] Define ICP, exclusions and audience segments.
- [ ] Verify consent, unsubscribe and suppression handling.
- [ ] Authenticate the sending domain and monitor reputation.
- [ ] Create campaign, contact, reply and enrollment fields in the CRM.
- [ ] Define ownership and response-time expectations for replies.
- [ ] Test links, personalization, sender identity, reply routing and mobile rendering.
- [ ] Establish the baseline and experiment hypothesis.

## During launch

- [ ] Monitor delivery failures, complaints and negative replies.
- [ ] Pause automation immediately when a human conversation begins.
- [ ] Record reply quality, objections and next actions.
- [ ] Check landing-page and application drop-off.
- [ ] Avoid changing multiple variables during an active test.

## After launch

- [ ] Reconcile replies, applications, enrollments and revenue.
- [ ] Review results by segment and acquisition source.
- [ ] Document what worked, failed or remained inconclusive.
- [ ] Transfer enrolled participants to onboarding.
- [ ] Update the next campaign brief without overwriting the preserved source.
